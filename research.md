My main area of interest is **analytic number theory**, especially $L$-functions, automorphic forms, and additive problems.

## L-functions and prime distribution

I am interested in the zeros and moments of the Riemann zeta-function, Dirichlet $L$-functions, and related families of $L$-functions, including how their analytic properties influence arithmetic estimates.

## Automorphic forms

My reading includes classical modular and automorphic forms, Hecke operators, the Petersson inner product, Kloosterman sums, and the Rankin–Selberg method.

## The circle method

I have studied the Hardy–Littlewood circle method through Waring's problem, including questions involving mixed powers. I am interested in exponential sums and the major-arc and minor-arc estimates that arise in additive number theory.
