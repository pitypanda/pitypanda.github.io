<p class="intro">I'm Jiacheng Sun, and I go by Tommy. I am a PhD student in mathematics at the University at Buffalo, with interests in analytic number theory.</p>

My interests include automorphic forms and $L$-functions, the distribution of primes, and the circle method. I am particularly interested in the connections between analytic methods and arithmetic problems.

## About me

I began my PhD at the University at Buffalo in August 2025. Before that, I completed my B.S. in Mathematics at the University of California, Davis in 2025, where I studied the circle method and Waring's problem.

Alongside my studies, I work as a teaching assistant for calculus. You can find more about my interests on the [Research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) page and my teaching on the [Teaching]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}) page.
